# Project__Sahaaye
SAHAAYE is an AI-powered social seva platform connecting real-world needs with people willing to help. It enables location-based discovery, photo/video reporting, smart search, AI-powered category suggestions, and face blurring for privacy, using a shared backend for Web and Android apps.



x# 🤝 Sahaaye — Discover. Connect. Contribute.

### Making social service opportunities easier to find.

Sahaaye is a platform designed to help people discover nearby social-service and volunteering opportunities based on their location and interests.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📍 Discover nearby social-service opportunities
- 🤝 Find ways to contribute to your community
- 🔎 Filter opportunities based on location and preferences
- 🌍 Make volunteering more accessible

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make social service easier to discover and encourage more people to contribute to their communities.
